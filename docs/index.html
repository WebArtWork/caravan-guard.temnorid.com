About the game

Defending trade caravans on dangerous roads.

A trade caravan sets out along a Temnorid road, and raiders, steppe riders and hungry wolves lie in wait. Place guards along the route, upgrade the ones that matter, and bring the wagons home.

The more goods arrive, the busier trade becomes in your city in Lords. A lost caravan means empty stalls.

Graphics are simple placeholder shapes for now. The game is built to be played first and polished later.

How to play

The first playable is one road, ten guard posts and three waves of attackers. You are a commander, not a fighter: you play from above and only buy and upgrade guards.

  1. You begin with 180 coins and a caravan of three wagons waiting at the start of the road.
  2. Click a free guard post beside the road and buy an archer for it (50 coins).
  3. Start a wave. The caravan moves on toward the next stopping point and attackers come for it from the sides; your archers shoot anyone in range.
  4. Every defeated attacker pays coins, and every cleared wave pays a bonus of 40. Spend them on new guards ahead of the caravan and on upgrades (40 coins: more damage, longer reach, faster shots).
  5. The caravan waits at each stopping point until the wave is dead. Clear all three waves and it reaches the market gate at the end of the road.

The caravan only moves while a wave is on, so guards left behind no longer help: each wave needs guards along the next stretch of road.

Playing with others

Up to four people defend the same caravan. On the first screen choose Play with others, then host a game (friends type the address it shows, or a short online code) or join one. Everybody shares one purse of coins and one caravan: anyone can place or upgrade guards or start a wave, and the host checks the cost and the post. Each camera is your own, and the others' pointers show on the map. The host decides the win or the loss; if the host leaves, the game ends for everybody.

Phones

Android and iOS builds play by touch: tap a post to select it, drag one finger to move the map, pinch to zoom, and use the big Buy / Upgrade and Start wave buttons. The places (Defence and Archers) and Profile sit in a bar at the bottom.

Updates

When a newer release is published on GitHub, the Windows game downloads and installs it by itself the next time it starts, and starts again. Turn it off in Profile if you prefer. On Android and iOS the update comes from the store or the release page.

Part of the world of Temnorid

Goods that arrive will raise market income and bring more merchants to your city in Lords.

The first time you press Play, you can sign in with the same Google account as in Lords, play as a guest, or play offline; Profile lets you change this later. Google sign-in is offered on Windows; phones offer guest only for now. The account gives the game your name and nothing else. Today Temnorid Caravan Guard does not talk to Lords. It does not need an account and works offline; only signing in, defending with friends and updates need an internet connection.
